# GAN Music Generator

**Short description:**  
This repo contains experiments with **GANs and diffusion models on music audio**, with a focus on using a **WGAN-GP discriminator as a “realism score”** to tell whether a track **sounds like real music or more like AI-generated audio**.

> In practice: we train a WGAN-GP on **real music only** (e.g. GTZAN), and then use the **critic/discriminator score** to compare **real vs. AI tracks** (e.g. Suno AI generations).

---

## Project Goals

- Learn a **latent space of realistic music** using **GANs / diffusion** on log-mel spectrograms.
- Use the **discriminator output as a realism score** for new tracks and investigate:
  - How much real songs score vs.
  - AI-generated songs (e.g. Suno / other models).
- Explore **codec parity** (WAV ↔ MP3) and **loudness normalization** to make the comparison fair.

---

## Main Components

### 1. `music_gan_realism.ipynb` (Main Notebook)

This is the **core notebook** of the project:

- Loads **real music** (e.g. GTZAN, Jazz subset).
- Converts audio into **log-mel spectrograms** with fixed shape `[1, 128, 256]`:
  - mono, resampled
  - mel-spectrogram
  - amplitude → dB
- Applies **loudness normalization**:
  - Preferably **LUFS (pyloudnorm)**; falls back to peak normalization if the library is missing.
- Optionally enforces **codec parity**:
  - Real WAVs are round-tripped through **MP3 (ffmpeg)** so real vs. AI are compared under similar compression.
- Trains a **WGAN-GP** with:
  - Gradient penalty
  - Spectral normalization on the discriminator
- After training, uses the **discriminator score as a “realism” score** for:
  - Held-out real tracks
  - AI-generated tracks (e.g., from Suno AI CAPS or other sources)
- Computes basic **evaluation metrics** (if `scikit-learn` is installed), such as:
  - ROC-AUC
  - PR-AUC

### 2. GAN Samples & Checkpoints

- `G.pth` – **Generator** checkpoint from WGAN-GP training.
- `D.pth` – **Discriminator / critic** checkpoint used as realism scorer.
- `generated_samples_gtzan/`
  - `v1_train_on_all_data/`
  - `v2_train_on_jazz_only/`
  - `v3_train_on_jazz_only_50_epochs/`
  - Contain `.wav` samples generated by the GAN under different training configs.

### 3. Diffusion / DDIM Experiments

- `ddimStudy.ipynb`  
  Early experiment on **generating music with GTZAN and Keras**, using spectrograms as images and a **DDIM-style denoising model**.
- `ddimgenPytorch.ipynb`  
  PyTorch version of the **DDIM-based music generation** idea.

These notebooks are more exploratory, comparing GAN vs. diffusion-style approaches.

### 4. Data Folders

- `jazz/` – A subset of GTZAN **Jazz** tracks in `.wav` format used for focused experiments.
- `ytclips/` – Small **YouTube clips** (`.webm`, `.mp3`) for quick testing and scoring.
- `runs/real_only_gan/` – **TensorBoard logs** (if you logged training) for the WGAN-GP experiments.

## Datasets

This project can use several public datasets:

- **GTZAN – Real Music Dataset (main “real” source)**  
  Music genre classification dataset, used here as **real human-made music**.  
  - [GTZAN Dataset on Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/andradaolteanu/gtzan-dataset-music-genre-classification)

- **Suno AI Caps – AI Music with Captions (AI examples)**  
  Captions + audio generations from Suno.  
  Used as **AI-generated music** examples to compare realism scores.  
  - [Suno AI Caps Kaggle Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/miguelcivit/sunocaps)

- **MusicCaps – Audio + Text Descriptions**  
  Text–audio pairs of music clips, useful for qualitative analysis, captions and future conditioning.  
  - [MusicCaps on HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/MusicCaps)
